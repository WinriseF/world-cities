# 2026-11-27 — major-city exceptions (0.1.133)

The owner approved major-city-priority, best-effort coverage for IQ, LR, ML, SY and TD instead of requiring complete statutory registers. Keep the previous city/municipality records and historical reference counts. The new `major-cities` status is intentionally distinct from `reference-checked` and `not-applicable`. No data change outside the five countries.

Compared the five existing shards with cached GeoNames country extracts for named populated places marked national or first-order capital or population at least 15,000 (excluding city sections and historical/abandoned settlements). Population estimates and former administrative classifications are screening evidence, not official current city status. Name, region and representative-point discrepancies were reviewed; same-name uncertainty, city subdivisions and non-independent suburbs were not automatically added. All five shards contain at least one location in each listed first-order region and records with Chinese and pinyin names.

## Changes

- **Liberia:** add the existing CSC/GeoNames settlements Yekepa (GeoNames 2272790) and Harbel (2276506) as searchable `locality` records, not new statutory municipalities. Both towns are named on the government-published public maps; Chinese names are GeoNames Chinese alias for Yekepa and project transliteration for Harbel. [Ministry of Agriculture national map](https://www.moa.gov.lr/sites/default/files/documents/ESMF%20%20STARP_Cleared_October%2012%202018%20%202-RSA.pdf), [LTA public map](https://lta.gov.lr/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ICT-Policy-of-Liberia-2019-2024.pdf).
- **Mali:** [2023 INSTAT locality directory](https://www.instat-mali.org/laravel-filemanager/files/shares/rgph/repertoire-localites-mali-rgph5_rgph.pdf) places Dioïla in Kaladougou, Fana in Guégnéka, Yanfolila in Wassoulou-Ballé, and Macina in Macina. Use the named main city as the visible English/Chinese label, retain the original legal commune names and spellings as searchable aliases, and use the named settlement representative point from the GeoNames public extract (IDs 2458747, 2457763, 2448518, 2455465 respectively), rather than the previous broad commune representative points. Preserve existing municipal IDs and legal names as aliases. Chinese aliases are project transliterations.
- **Iraq:** add geographically matched alternate Latin spellings for Akre, Dereluk and Sheladiza (GeoNames 445694, 9253107, 13680586), without changing the existing representative points.
- **Chad:** add the Bébédja orthographic variants for already present Bebidja. No coordinate change.
- **Syria:** retain existing records. No promotion of historically ambiguous classifications or automatic import of peri-urban subdivisions.

The full legal-register count remains 229/234; the five explicit exceptions are now accepted for best-effort major-city coverage. No implied 100% completeness of all world cities. Historical partial-roster review retained in the 2026-11-25 audit. [GeoNames data and CC BY license](https://www.geonames.org/export/).
