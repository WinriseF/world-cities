# Sources for the city and province additions

This edition matches records to national geographic identifiers, selects representative city coordinates, normalizes names and adds Mandarin readings. The packaged JSON is a derived dataset. Dates below identify the registers used to establish membership.

| Coverage | Attribution and source |
| --- | --- |
| Brazilian municipal seats and Brasília | Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística, [municipalities API](https://servicodados.ibge.gov.br/api/v1/localidades/municipios?view=nivelado) and [Localidades do Brasil 2022, national GeoPackage](https://geoftp.ibge.gov.br/organizacao_do_territorio/estrutura_territorial/localidades/Localidades_do_Brasil/2022/Localidades_Brasil_gpkg.zip). |
| United States incorporated cities | U.S. Census Bureau, [2025 National Places Gazetteer](https://www.census.gov/geographies/reference-files/time-series/geo/gazetteer-files.2025.html). Selected city equivalents include consolidated governments. City-status exceptions include [Manchester](https://www.city-manchester.org/1231/About-Manchester), [Walker](https://www.walker.la.us/), [Livingston](https://www.cityoflivingston-tx.com/148/City-Council), [Talty](https://www.taltytexas.com/page/departments), [Venus](https://www.cityofvenus.org/) and [Yakutat](https://www.yakutatak.us/). |
| German cities holding Stadtrecht | Statistisches Bundesamt (Destatis), [Städte am 31.12.2024](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Laender-Regionen/Regionales/Gemeindeverzeichnis/Administrativ/05-staedte.html). Includes the subsequent [grant of city status to Haar](https://www.stadt-haar.de/aktuelles/Wir-sind-Stadt). The roster retains Hanau's reference-year code; the [Hessen register for 2026](https://statistik.hessen.de/sites/statistik.hessen.de/files/2026-01/verz-2_26-01.pdf) links the new code. |
| Japanese cities | Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications, [national local government codes](https://www.soumu.go.jp/denshijiti/code.html), municipality sheet dated 2024-01-01. Tokyo is retained separately. |
| Philippine cities | Philippine Statistics Authority, [Philippine Standard Geographic Code city list](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/cities) and [2026 summary](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/summary). Uses city codes and the two-digit region prefix. [PSA terms](https://psa.gov.ph/terms-of-use). |
| Province and territorial corrections | [Burundi presidency](https://presidence.gov.bi/le-pays/), [Burundi statistics office](https://insbudemo.insbu.bi/PopulationDataPage), [Angola division act](https://minea.gov.ao/media/uploads/Lei_14_24_de_5_de_Setembro_Cubango_1555887991.pdf), [French INSEE geographic code 2026](https://www.insee.fr/fr/information/8740222), [UK Cabinet Office city list](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/list-of-cities/list-of-cities-html) and [China Ministry of Commerce, Burundi guide](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/dl/gbdqzn/upload/bulongdi.pdf). |
| International urban points | [UN Statistics Division, Demographic Yearbook 2024, table 8](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/demographic-social/products/dyb/dyb_2024/) is used for cross-checking. Representative coordinates for the corroborated cities are from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/export/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Individual IDs are recorded in the repository in sources/reviewed-world-cities.json. |
| Identifiers and names | [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing), CC0 structured data accessed through [QLever](https://qlever.dev/wikidata). Linked on the nationally assigned identifier, using P1585 (IBGE), P774 (FIPS), P439 (German municipality code), P429 (Japanese local government code) and P988 (PSGC). Names and coordinates are also cross-checked against the sources listed in NOTICE.md. |

Poland uses the Polish Head Office of Geodesy and Cartography's official PRNG miasto points and Statistics Poland's January 2026 TERYT urban-unit codes. The province uses the first two TERYT digits. Each of the 1,026 cities matches the official name and urban code in both registers. Supplemental Chinese labels were matched by Wikidata P4046 (SIMC locality identifier) and proximity to the PRNG point. [PRNG](https://www.geoportal.gov.pl/en/data/national-register-of-geographical-names/) · [Statistics Poland, KTS and TERYT](https://stat.gov.pl/en/regional-statistics/classification-of-territorial-units/the-coding-system-for-territorial-and-statistical-units-kts/kts-units-and-their-symbols/).

Indonesia uses the Geospatial Information Agency's administrative polygons attribute table, the municipality class tipadm=5 and the Ministry of Home Affairs' codes. The 2025 ministry total is 98, including five administrative cities in Jakarta. Jakarta as a whole remains searchable in the province list. Five new representative points use GeoNames (Subulussalam 6713355, Sawahlunto 1628472, Bitung 1648637, Baubau 1649541, and Tidore's seat Soasio 1623951). Their Chinese names use Wikidata Q5789, Q7263, Q15845, Q15378 and Q19153, respectively. [BIG administrative layer](https://geoservices.big.go.id/rbi/rest/services/BATASWILAYAH/BATAS_WILAYAH/MapServer/13) · [Ministry's 2025 report](https://binapemdes.kemendagri.go.id/publikasi%20lainnya/LAKIN%202025%20Ditjen%20Bina%20Pemdes.pdf) · [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/).

The previous spatial audit's confirmed changes to seven city coordinates and seven parent assignments use IBGE municipality seats, GeoNames and the national or local authorities listed in the [archived audit findings](https://github.com/WinriseF/world-cities/blob/9ee4670ec4ae32016611c0283efc6033fe15522c/audit/2026-11-20/gadm-verified-findings.csv). The independent national sources supply the values used in this release.

Further national registers, editions, record scopes and attribution appear in the [November 2026 national review](national-review.md).
