Papua New Guinea: the National Statistical Office's 22 final 2024 census provincial workbooks (table 1, issued November 2026) identify 35 urban local-level governments. Bougainville's Executive Council documents four statutory urban community governments separately. Port Moresby is the single National Capital District Commission city. The 2026 Treasury grants and the national organic law corroborate the roster. Places are located with GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), with Kokopau from the national gazetted road register and provinces checked against NSO boundaries from OCHA HDX. Bangladesh: 330 municipal towns and 13 city corporations, covering 342 distinct locations after joining Dhaka's two corporations. The 2024 administrator gazette and two related gazettes confirm the 330-name starting roster, including Narayanpur in Chandpur. The 2026 gazettes add Pekua and incorporate Bogura into its city corporation. English spellings were checked against the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics urban report and locally gazetted names. Representative points use OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), BBS boundary data published through geoBoundaries, and the Bangladeshi government facilities registers. Saudi Arabia: The ministry's penalties and service-fee tables identify 285 municipal authorities across all 13 provinces. Dammam, Khobar and Dhahran are explicitly identified as separate cities under the Eastern Province Amanah. The list has 287 locations after retaining one point for Hafar Al-Batin and omitting the historical Abu Rakah code. The points are from government municipality directories, OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and documented municipal addresses. The audit identifies Chinese phonetic renderings where an established label was unavailable.
